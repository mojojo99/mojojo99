## Leadership Training

### 1. Emerging Leaders Programme (2 days)
- **For:** New managers, high-potential individual contributors
- **Group size:** 12–24 pax
- **Format:** In-person workshop, 9am–5pm
- **Covers:** Transitioning from peer to leader, situational leadership,
  giving feedback, delegation, coaching conversations
- **Price:** SGD 8,800 per run (up to 24 pax), excludes venue

### 2. Executive Leadership Intensive (3 days)
- **For:** Senior managers, directors, C-suite
- **Group size:** 8–16 pax
- **Format:** In-person retreat style, can be run off-site
- **Covers:** Strategic thinking, leading change, executive presence,
  stakeholder influence, building high-performing teams
- **Price:** SGD 18,000 per run, excludes venue and accommodation

### 3. Leadership Coaching (1-on-1)
- **For:** Individual leaders at any level
- **Format:** 6 × 90-minute sessions over 3 months, in-person or virtual
- **Price:** SGD 4,500 per person

## Teambuilding

### 4. Team Synergy Day (1 day)
- **For:** Intact teams or departments, 15–60 pax
- **Format:** Indoor/outdoor experiential activities + debrief
- **Covers:** Trust, communication, collaboration under pressure
- **Price:** SGD 120 per pax (min 15 pax), excludes venue

### 5. Amazing-Race Style Challenge (half day)
- **For:** Company events, D&Ds, offsites, 20–200 pax
- **Format:** Outdoor city challenge with facilitators
- **Price:** SGD 80 per pax (min 20 pax)

### 6. Custom Team Retreats (1–3 days)
- **For:** Leadership teams, project teams
- **Format:** Fully customised — mix of workshops and experiential activities
- **Price:** From SGD 15,000, quoted after a discovery call

## Discounts & Terms
- 10% discount for bookings of 2 or more programmes in the same quarter
- 50% deposit to confirm booking; balance due 7 days before event
- Free reschedule up to 14 days before the event; 50% fee within 14 days
- All prices exclude GST/VAT unless stated otherwise
